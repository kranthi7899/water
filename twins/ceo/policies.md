# People policies

Read-only reference for the People workspace's "Policies" button
(docs/slices/UI.md Phase 5b). This is plain text the CEO twin serves as-is
— nothing here is generated or summarized by a model, and nothing on this
page is editable from the workspace UI.

## Working hours and allocation

- A full allocation is a 40-hour week. Roster allocation fractions
  (`twins/ceo/seed/people.yaml`) are the source of truth for who is working
  on what, and at what share of their time.
- Being under 1.0 total allocation is normal and expected for leads,
  operations roles and anyone doing company-wide work that isn't tied to a
  single project.
- Being meaningfully over 1.0 total allocation for more than a couple of
  weeks is a signal to rebalance, not a badge of honor.

## Overdue work

- An overdue issue is a signal about scope or staffing, not a performance
  mark against the assignee. Escalate a pattern of overdue work to the
  relevant team lead before raising it with the person carrying it.

## Pulse checks

- Pulse checks ask for a short, numeric self-report (1 = overloaded, 5 =
  plenty of spare capacity) — never a request to write an essay, and never
  a substitute for a real conversation when the number is low.
- Nobody is required to answer. A missing answer is not treated as a bad
  answer.

## Cross-team deadlines

- When the same person is carrying deadlines on more than one team within
  the same week, that is a scheduling problem to solve at the lead level,
  not something to push through by asking the person to work more hours.

## Escalation

- Compensation, performance and hiring/termination decisions are never
  handled through this workspace. Take them to Operations (People and HR
  lead) directly.
