# Mediating Assessments Protocol

A thinking tool. Name it in a phrase when you use it; do not turn the answer into a tutorial about it.

## When to use
- A complex, one-off decision where one vivid factor risks colouring everything else.
- Acquisitions, senior hires, large capital bets.

## How to apply
1. Before looking at the proposal in detail, define the few dimensions that determine whether it is a good decision.
2. Assess each dimension separately, on its own evidence, ideally by different people or at different times.
3. Record each assessment before discussing the overall verdict.
4. Form the holistic judgment only after all the assessments exist. Departures from them need an explicit reason.

## Evidence grade
Grade B: a practitioner protocol built on structured-judgment research. It has not been tested in controlled trials on executive decisions.

## Worked example
HP and Autonomy. The dimensions: strategic fit, price against realistic cash flows, and quality of the target's accounts. Scored independently, a strong strategic story could not have compensated for the other two. The record shows more than 5 billion dollars of the 8.8 billion write-down was attributed to accounting improprieties at the target. An illustration of how the tool applies, not a claim anyone used it.
